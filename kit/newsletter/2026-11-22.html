01 · THE WEEK IN ONE LINE

HARDEN'S 51, SGA'S 37 A NIGHT

James Harden scored 51 in Cleveland's 128–119 win over Indiana on Friday, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander averaged 37.0 points over three games, and Denver came back from 17 down to beat Boston.

02 · GAME OF THE WEEK

FINAL · WED, NOV 18 · DENVER

NUGGETS 105
CELTICS 101

★ FEATURED RECAP

Boston led 61–44 late in the second quarter. Denver won the third 31–19, the lead changed hands 15 times in the final 16 minutes, and Jamal Murray's 17-foot pull-up with 6.4 seconds left made it 104–101.

The whole game, in motion

THREE THINGS THAT DECIDED IT

1  THE THIRD QUARTER. Down 12 at the half, Denver won the third 31–19 while Boston turned it over on eight straight possessions.

2  THE FREE-THROW LINE. Denver made 24 free throws to Boston’s 10. The Celtics were called for 30 fouls to Denver’s 13.

3  DEROZAN, THEN MURRAY. DeMar DeRozan scored 13 of his 18 in the fourth, including the free throws with 49 seconds left that put Denver ahead for good, 102–101. Murray’s jumper sealed it.

04 · DEEP DIVES

★ HOTTEST PLAYER · THUNDER

SHAI GILGEOUS-ALEXANDER

The league’s hottest scorer this week: 37.0 points a game over three games on 56 percent shooting, and the Thunder won two of the three. His best was Friday: 40 points on 14-of-24 shooting as Oklahoma City came back from 16 down to beat New Orleans 126–120.

His signature move is the hard-stop pull-up: full speed at the rim so his defender backpedals, one hard stop, and up into the gap before the defender can close it.

▲ COACH'S BOARD · KNICKS

BRUNSON’S CORNER FADEAWAY

Tied 106–106 in overtime at Toronto last Sunday, 35 seconds left, Mike Brown’s Knicks came out of a timeout with Karl-Anthony Towns screening for Jalen Brunson, who ran to the left corner. Mikal Bridges found him from under the basket, and Brunson’s fadeaway three with 33 seconds left put New York ahead for good. Knicks 112, Raptors 108.
